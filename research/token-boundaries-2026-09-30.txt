Concrete Hopper changes from this review

1. TokenBatch waived duplicate writable-account checks for the entire batch.
   That is necessary across separate inner instructions, but also let a
   single self-transfer bypass the individual builder's refusal. Validate
   the newly appended instruction only; preserve cross-instruction reuse.
2. TokenInstruction is an open trait. A custom encoder may return an error
   after emitting a prefix. Checkpoint the whole push and restore its visible
   lengths on error, including the multisig path.
3. ExtraAccountMetaList::unpack ignored the TLV discriminator and value
   length, reading its count against the whole account. Select Execute's
   stable discriminator, walk preceding TLVs, and bound the count by the
   selected value. This does not validate the account's owner or PDA.
4. Reserved entry discriminators 3..127 could underflow the external-program
   index; excessive seed counts/lengths could reach a panicking search.
   Return errors, reserve a bump slot, and use a fallible PDA search.
5. Expose the existing native fallible canonical search through the ordinary
   runtime PDA module as try_find_program_address returning Result. No heap,
   no new off-chain service, and no claim to have invented PDA derivation.

Limits still open

The hook resolver's address-only API does not implement AccountData seeds or
pubkey-data entries, nor does it provide a complete hook-program authoring
interface. These require owner/provenance and borrow-aware account data APIs,
not silently guessed addresses. Token batches have a 255-byte inner-payload
wire limit and reject within-instruction writable aliases. Individual APIs
remain necessary for larger payloads and legitimate aliasing instructions.
No universal fastest, safest, drop-in replacement, or full-token-coverage
claim follows from this work. The broader 0.5 release remains coordinated
because already-landed public signatures break the published 0.4 line.
