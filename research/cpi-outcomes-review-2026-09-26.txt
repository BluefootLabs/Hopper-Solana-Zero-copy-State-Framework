Read Pina crates/pina/src/token.rs verified module: scalar debit/credit arithmetic
requires positive exact debit and positive credit no larger than the amount.
The consumer supplies identity and lower-bound policy. Hopper's existing scalar
balance helpers also leave these responsibilities to callers. Add a separate
snapshot bound to actual account views, with an explicit minimum receipt and
fresh owner, shape, mint, state and authority checks. Do not claim this general
before/after pattern is a novel invention. The useful improvement is reducing
application mistakes while preserving choice of CPI builder.

Read canonical Token-2022 processor transfer section, pinned at
23b03f88e44a9ef2587e5a08206a09eca0c8f17b: self-transfer returns without moving
balances after validation; ordinary transfer debits the requested amount and
credits amount minus calculated fee, accumulating withheld fees separately.
These are distinct invariants. Net base-balance checks do not prove internal
attribution or replace extension policy. No upstream implementation copied.

Hopper runtime CPI dedup implementation and native descriptor/preflight paths
were reviewed for account matching and borrow retention. Reordered dedup infos
were validated by pubkey but host System transfer emulation used positions 0/1.
The baseline debited an unrelated extra account. Resolve both metas by pubkey
before host emulation. On-chain syscall resolution already uses addresses.

Miri on the token tests found a separate host RefMut provenance defect: deriving
a pointer from a mutable reborrow and then moving its native parent guard retags

retain a raw pointer and one release obligation, and move the lease through
projections. The SBF representation already uses raw parts. A second Miri error
was in the new Box-backed test fixture, fixed with a Vec allocation pointer;
that fixture error is not a product defect.
